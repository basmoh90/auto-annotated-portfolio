💡 USAGE TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For Best Results:
  - Use 3-7 tags per post (not too many)
  - Use consistent tag names across posts
  - Add 'keywords' custom field with 3-5 keywords
  - Start with Max Related Posts = 10
  - Enable "Link in Headings" only if needed
  - Clear cache monthly for maintenance
  - Monitor post save times

Performance:
  - Small sites (<100 posts): Use default settings
  - Medium sites (100-1K posts): Max 10-15 related posts
  - Large sites (1K+ posts): Max 5-10, enable object cache
